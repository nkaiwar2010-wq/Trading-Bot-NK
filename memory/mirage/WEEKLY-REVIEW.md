# Mirage Weekly Review

Appended every Friday afternoon. Purpose: a skimmable, honest scorecard —
win rate, average win/loss size, total realized P&L for the week — not
just a list of trades. This is the trust-building record: real, realized
(not unrealized/hoped-for) results, reviewed on a fixed schedule whether
the week was good or bad.

Format each entry:

## Week of YYYY-MM-DD (Mon) to YYYY-MM-DD (Fri)

### Scorecard
- Trades opened: N | Trades closed: N (should be equal — Mirage never
  holds overnight)
- Wins: N | Losses: N | Win rate: X%
- Average win: $X | Average loss: $X | Win/loss size ratio: X:1
- Total realized P&L this week: ±$X (±X%)
- Phase P&L (since $50,000 start): ±$X (±X%)

### What worked
- Plain-english: which setups/symbols/times of day produced the wins

### What didn't
- Plain-english: repeated loss patterns, if any (a specific catalyst
  type, a time-of-day, entries without a clean ORB confirmation, etc.)

### Verdict
- Honest one-line read: is this strategy showing real edge, too early to
  tell, or showing a pattern that needs fixing? Do not oversell a small
  sample — a few good or bad days is not proof either way this early.

### Adjustment (if any)
- Any rule change being proposed for STRATEGY.md as a result, or "none
  yet — sample still too small."
